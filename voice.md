# Eventually — Voice

## Voice vs tone

**Voice** (always): calm, sharp, mischievous, deadpan.
**Tone** (shifts): wry on the homepage; clearer and warmer in product/email; plain and precise in legal/support.

## Address

**Default: you.** Speak to the person with the overflowing bookmarks.

Use **we** sparingly — ownership and mechanics only (“we email you…”, “we built Eventually…”). Avoid corporate “we’re excited to announce.”

## Personality in language

| Trait | Sounds like | Doesn’t sound like |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| Calm | steady, small, snackable, when you’re ready | hustle, grind, don’t miss out |
| Sharp | one click, one email, works with X bookmarks | synergy, seamless ecosystem |
| Mischievous | balloons, “stupid-simple,” a dry wink | forced jokes, meme-speak |
| Deadpan | state the absurdity flat | exclamation salads!!! |

Humor: **both** dry wink *and* occasional absurdity (balloon-level) are allowed — never at the expense of clarity.

## We love

- revisit
- past gems
- later
- saved / bookmarks
- calm, daily, steady, snackable
- dusty pile → inbox
- “oh right, that thread!”

## We ban

- AI-powered
- productivity
- inbox zero
- unlock
- also avoid: game-changer, leverage, seamless, next-gen, optimize your workflow, revolutionary

## Messaging pillars

1. **Recognition** — You save more than you revisit.
2. **Promise** — Later should keep its promise.
3. **Mechanism** — A tiny extension → a calm email of past gems.
4. **Anti-complexity** — No dashboards, no complicated filters.

## Value prop (canonical)

Bet you have hundreds of saved tweets. Use Eventually to actually revisit them. A tiny browser extension that emails you the tweets you meant to come back to, but never did.

## Tagline

Crafted for people with overflowing bookmark folders.

## CTA patterns

| Context | Prefer | Avoid |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Primary | Download Chrome Extension · Get Eventually | Unlock now · Start free trial (unless true) |
| Setup reassurance | Takes a few minutes to set up. | Instant AI magic |
| Compatibility | Works with X bookmarks. · Safari & Firefox coming soon | Works everywhere!! |

When Safari/Firefox ship, name them plainly — same calm voice, no fanfare inflation.

## Microcopy do / don’t

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| “Emails you the tweets you meant to come back to” | “Supercharge your content workflow” |
| “Stupid-simple: one click to bookmark, one email to revisit” | “All-in-one productivity hub” |
| “Your personal later that eventually shows up” | “Never forget anything again” (overclaim) |

## Channel shifts

| Channel | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Homepage / marketing | Conversational, memorable, allowed weird |
| Extension UI | Short, calm, instructional |
| Reminder email | Soft, clear, snackable; one job per mail |
| Support | Empathetic, plain, no jokes that obscure the fix |
| Legal | Formal, precise; brand voice yields to clarity |

## Company naming

Public brand: **Eventually**.
Legal entity (later): Becomes Tech LLC — use in footer/legal only when ready; don’t force into marketing voice.

## Voice test

1. Would Pocket or Notion say this the same way? If yes → sharpen.
2. Does it make a busy X saver nod “yeah, that’s me”?
3. Is it calm *and* specific — not vague positivity?
